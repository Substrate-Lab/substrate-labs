Research

What we work on.

We study the structure of intelligence in machines, in molecules, and in the systems that shape our world.

Machines

Alignment

Closing the gap between what capable models are asked to do and what they actually do, and keeping it correctable.

Machines

Interpretability

Reverse-engineering the features and circuits behind model behavior, so internals can be checked, not assumed.

Molecules

Biology

Probabilistic methods for molecular systems, from nucleation pathways to the behavior of biological compounds.

Systems

Societal

How deployment and governance choices shape the real-world impact of capable AI.

Policy

Shaping the rules.

We work with the bodies writing the rules for AI and security, at the state, federal, and international level. Our aim is narrow and practical: turn vague legal standards into tests that can actually be run.

Regulatory comments
  • State

    Colorado Attorney General

    Pre-rulemaking comment on the Automated Decision-Making Technology Act (SB 26-189) and the Chatbot Safety Act (HB 26-1263), proposing measurable standards for the statutes' vague operative terms.

    Filed2026
  • Federal

    Federal Trade Commission

    Comment on the proposed Policy Statement on the Suppression of Accuracy in AI Systems (Docket FTC-2026-0859), arguing that calibration and honest abstention are themselves forms of accuracy, and that the deception test should rest on measurement.

    Filed2026
